Contact Us

If you have any questions or need assistance, please feel free to contact our call center. Our friendly staff is available to help you with any inquiries or concerns you may have.

To elevate customer care and streamline remote shopping, our super shop features a dedicated, state-of-the-art call center facility operating right at the heart of our service network. This infrastructure is equipped with advanced telecommunication systems, automated interactive voice response (IVR) software, and high-speed databases integrated directly with our store inventory. A highly trained team of customer service representatives handles incoming calls to assist shoppers with order placements, track home deliveries, check real-time stock availability, and manage membership loyalty rewards. By providing an immediate, direct line of communication, the call center ensures that elderly shoppers, busy professionals, and homebound customers can enjoy a frictionless retail experience without stepping foot inside the physical store.

In addition to voice support, our call center is equipped with a multi-channel communication system that includes email, live chat, and social media integration. This allows customers to reach out through their preferred method of communication, ensuring that assistance is always accessible. The call center also maintains a comprehensive knowledge base and FAQ section, enabling customers to find quick answers to common questions. By leveraging technology and human expertise, our call center plays a crucial role in enhancing customer satisfaction and loyalty, making it an indispensable part of the super shop's commitment to exceptional service.
